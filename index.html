Hello, I’m kathyjenner, a romance novelist. I build stories between the real and the imagined, drawn to the choices people make, the ways they change, and the quiet truths of human emotion.

01 / ABOUT ME

Different worlds.
True emotions.

I create authentic, layered characters and write about love, growth, and choice across genres.

I’m interested in the ways relationships become part of a character’s life and evolution. Beyond writing, I’m also a software engineer—an experience that brings another way of observing, questioning, and thinking to my creative work.
